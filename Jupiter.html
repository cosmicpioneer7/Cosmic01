Jupiter Fact Sheet

Equatorial Radius 71,492 km
Surface Gravity 24.79 m/s² (2.52g)
Distance to Sun 778.5 M km (5.2 AU)
Orbital Period 11.86 years
Cloud Top Temp -108°C
Estimated Age ~4.6 billion years
Atmosphere 89% H₂, 10% He

Jupiter is a massive gas giant, famous for its intricate swirling cloud bands and the Great Red Spot—a gigantic storm that has been raging for centuries.

It lacks a solid surface and acts somewhat like a miniature solar system, hosting at least 95 known moons, including the massive Galilean satellites.
